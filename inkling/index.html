A drawing journal for life's moments

AI draws the first line. You decide the rest.

Some moments are hard to put into words: a beginning, a loss, a small win, a strange Tuesday. Inkling gives you a first mark and a prompt to start from. How the drawing grows, what it means, and when it feels finished are up to you.

AI

It offers a way in

Tell it what happened and how it feels. It suggests three starting marks, each with a short prompt. You pick one, or start blank.

You

You develop it

Draw over it, around it, or erase it. The AI stays quiet. If you get stuck, you can ask for a question, never an instruction.

You

You decide when it's enough

Before you start, you choose what you want from the drawing. At the end, you judge it against that, and keep going if it isn't there yet.

Your drawings and notes stay in this browser. To write prompts, Inkling sends only the type of moment, your feelings and your optional one-line note.
